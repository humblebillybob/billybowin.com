Emplifi Creative Workflow Process

Design Studio Management System

Building Emplifi’s brand was only half the job. The other half was building the operational system that would make the creative team function like an internal agency—at scale, with consistency, without the administrative overhead quietly eating into the team’s capacity.

We architected a five-stage end-to-end creative workflow: a Design Studio Portal for structured request intake, an auto-incrementing job numbering system with 25+ asset category codes, Asana-integrated sprint management, standardized Creative Cloud folder and file naming conventions, a job completion and packaging protocol, and a Google Drive archive system linked back through a live database.

The result: a 50%+ reduction in administrative and distribution costs, clearer accountability, and a creative team that could handle higher volume without adding headcount.

AcousticDoodler

AI-Assisted Development, Interactive Application

AcousticDoodler started as a deliberate experiment: how far could I push Claude.ai as a development partner before I hit a wall that required writing code myself? The answer was all the way to a production-ready, publicly deployed interactive music education platform—without writing a single line of code.

My contribution to the build was a UI mockup, which I fed to Claude as the design guide. Everything that followed—architecture decisions, front-end implementation, interactivity, and deployment—was driven entirely through prompt-based development.

The project proved out something I now apply across my team: AI-assisted development is not a shortcut for developers. It is a legitimate production methodology that puts build capability in the hands of people who think in systems and design, not just those who write syntax.
